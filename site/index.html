CUEd for Android

An offline music player with a real crossfade, a live spectrograph, lyrics, and sharing that never touches a server. No accounts. No cloud. No AI.

Download the APK All releases
Android 8.0 or newer. Scan the code with your phone to download directly.

Install and update

  1. Download the APK (button or QR above) and open it. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser or file manager.
  2. On first launch, allow access to your music. CUEd scans what is already on the phone.
  3. Updating: Settings → Updates → Check for updates. CUEd downloads the new APK, verifies its SHA-256 against the published checksum, then hands it to Android's installer. It also checks once a day by itself and shows a banner on Home. No more trips to this page.
Builds are signed with a public dev key so they install over each other. Only install APKs from this page or the GitHub releases.

Playing

Gestures on the artworkSwipe left/right for next/previous. Tap to play or pause. Double-tap to favourite. Hold for the track menu. Swipe up for sing-along lyrics.
Mini playerSwipe the strip left or right to skip. Tap to open the player.
ScrubberA plain bar or a live spectrograph drawn from the audio itself (no microphone). Choose in Settings → Scrubber. Tap or drag to seek.
CrossfadeSettings → Crossfade: on by default, 1–20 s, equal-power / linear / smooth curve. Off means gapless.
Tempo-match crossfadeOff by default, separate switch. Matches the incoming track's beat grid at 1:1, 2:1, 3:2, 4:3… whichever needs the least stretch, and glides both tracks to the new tempo. Falls back to the normal crossfade when tempos don't relate.
Skip silenceSettings → Silence: cuts quiet stretches longer than a tolerance. Position stays accurate.

Library, genres, playlists

  • Library has four views, and the search box narrows whichever one is open: Artists (with an A–Z rail) → an artist page with albums, singles & EPs, loose tracks and "appears on" → a numbered album page; Albums as a grid; Tracks; Genres. Every track's menu has "Go to artist" and "Go to album". Grouping follows the album-artist tag, then the name before "feat.", so guest spots land on both artists' pages.
  • Home shows Trending, Newly downloaded, Unplayed, Forgotten, Favourites and Recommended. Every list is a rule over your own listening history, readable in the source. Any list can be saved as a playlist.
  • Genres are read from each file's tag and tidied ("Hip-Hop", "hiphop" and "(7)" all become hip hop). Settings → Genres can re-read tags, tidy labels, or fill gaps from MusicBrainz (opt-in). Tracks you edit by hand are never re-labelled. The Library screen shows an unlabelled chip while anything is missing a genre.
  • Playlists are hand-curated: create, reorder, rename, add from any track's menu.

Downloading

Share a link to CUEd (or open it with CUEd) from Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, Deezer, Tidal, SoundCloud, Bandcamp or Amazon Music, or type a search. It lands in the download box with what was detected: a track, an album or a playlist. Three backends, chosen under Downloads → Backend:

Built-in (default)No Python, nothing to install. Spotify or YouTube links and searches are matched on YouTube Music and saved as m4a or mp3 (your choice) with cover, tags and track numbers. Optional Spotify developer keys unlock full playlists and genres; without them a keyless fallback reads tracks and albums.
Termux on the phoneRuns spotdl inside Termux. Setup once: pkg install python ffmpeg, pip install spotdl, termux-setup-storage, and allow-external-apps=true in ~/.termux/termux.properties; then grant CUEd the "Run commands in Termux" permission. If Termux upgrades its Python later and spotdl breaks with "libpython… not found", Downloads → Repair spotdl reinstalls it.
Companion on a laptop or Pipip install spotdl, then python3 tools/spotdl-server/server.py (or run.bat / run.sh) on any machine on your Wi-Fi, and enter its address. Files are pulled over when done.

Other platforms' tracks and albums are matched through song.link; Deezer playlists are expanded track by track. Apple Music, Tidal, SoundCloud and Amazon playlists can't be read without an account, so share those tracks one at a time. Two checkboxes control lyrics with downloads: save .lrc files beside tracks, and look lyrics up in CUEd afterwards.

Each backend has a Test button on the Downloads page. If YouTube ever answers "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot", update CUEd first (its YouTube client follows the NewPipe project's), then try the other network (mobile data vs Wi-Fi).

Lyrics

Swipe up on the artwork (or tap the lyric line under the scrubber). Sources, in order: lyrics embedded in the file's tag (spotdl writes these), a .lrc or .txt beside the file, then lrclib.net if allowed in Settings → Lyrics. Synced lyrics scroll with the song; tap a line to jump there. Settings → Lyrics can fetch for the whole library.

Sharing tracks and the app

Track menu → Share. The phone shows a QR code and, if NFC is on, waits to be tapped by another phone on its Receive screen (sidebar → Receive a share). The code carries the track's source link, a local file URL served by the phone while the Share screen is open, and a link to install CUEd itself. Nothing goes through a server.

Car mode and voice

Sidebar (☰) → Car mode: one screen of oversized controls, quick-play tiles and a big Voice button, with spoken replies. It can switch on by itself when Android reports a car dock. Android Auto shows CUEd as a media app with a browse tree (enable "Unknown sources" in the Android Auto app's developer settings, since CUEd isn't on the Play Store).

Voice works from the mic button, from Google Assistant ("play forgotten tracks on CUEd") and from Android Auto. Understanding is rule-based: "play something", "play my gym playlist", "play some house", "play songs by Daft Punk", "next", "shuffle off", "car mode", "what's playing".

Widget and lock screen

Long-press the home screen → Widgets → CUEd. Resize it: one row is a bar with play/pause and skip, two rows add the artist, three or more show the cover large. Tapping the cover opens Now Playing.

Settings → Lock screen player puts CUEd's own full-screen player above the keyguard while music plays: clock, cover, scrubber, big controls. Swipe down to hide, or tap "Unlock and open". Android 10+ needs "display over other apps" for this; Settings has the button, and CUEd uses that permission for nothing else.

Podcasts and audiobooks

Anything over 12 minutes is kept out of the music library and lives under Sidebar → Podcasts & audiobooks. It resumes where you stopped, gets back-10 / forward-30 and speed controls, and is never crossfaded, recommended, or queued unless you pick it or put it in a playlist. Misfiled? "Move to Music" is in the track menu.

Getting around

The bottom bar is Library · Home · Playlists. The strip above it is the mini player: it stays on every screen while you browse or search, swipe it to skip, tap it for the full player. The side menu (☰) holds Downloads, Podcasts & audiobooks, Car mode, Voice, Receive a share, Settings and Report a bug. A badge appears in the top bar while downloads are running.

Reporting a bug

Settings → Report a bug, the Report button on a failed download, or the banner Home shows after a crash. You write a line or two; CUEd adds the app and Android version, which features are on, and (if you leave the box ticked) the recent debug log with tokens and keys redacted. "Show what gets sent" previews it. It becomes a public issue on GitHub: posted straight from the app if you've saved a GitHub token under Settings → Updates, otherwise the prefilled issue form opens in your browser and you submit it there. Turn on Settings → Debug log before reproducing a problem so the log has something in it.

What leaves the phone

NeverListening history, library, playlists, recommendations. All local, no account, no telemetry.
Your LAN onlyThe share server (while the Share screen is open) and the companion downloader.
On requestDownloads: YouTube Music and Spotify's embed page or API (built-in backend), or spotdl's own traffic (Termux / companion); song.link and Deezer's API to resolve shared links. lrclib.net (lyrics, toggle). MusicBrainz (genres, opt-in). api.github.com for update checks (toggle) and for bug reports, only when you press the button.

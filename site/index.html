How to use it

Five minutes from download to playing.

  1. InstallOpen the downloaded file. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser. Updates come from inside the app afterwards: Settings → Updates.
  2. Let it see your musicOn first launch, allow access to audio. CUEd scans what is already on the phone and sorts it by artist, album, track and genre.
  3. PlayTap any track. The bottom bar is Library · Home · Playlists, and the mini player above it follows you everywhere. Tap it for the full player: swipe the cover to skip, tap to pause, hold for the menu, swipe up for lyrics. Hold any song in a list for its options, including deleting it from the phone.
  4. Download a songShare a Spotify or YouTube link to CUEd from any app, or type a search in the side menu → Downloads. It arrives with cover, tags and lyrics.
  5. Share with a friendTrack menu → Share shows a QR code, or tap phones with NFC. They get the song and a link to this app. Nothing goes through a server.
CrossfadeOn by default in Settings. The tempo-matched version is a separate switch for mixes that follow the beat.
Car mode and voiceSide menu → Car mode for big controls. Say "play some house" or "next" from the mic button or Google Assistant.
Widget and lock screenLong-press the home screen → Widgets → CUEd. Settings → Lock screen player puts CUEd above the keyguard.
Podcasts and audiobooksAnything over 12 minutes lives in its own section, resumes where you stopped, and never sneaks into the music queue.
Something broke?Settings → Report a bug sends the details, with nothing you didn't choose to include.
PrivacyNo account, no telemetry. The app only goes online for the things you ask it to: downloads, lyrics, updates.
